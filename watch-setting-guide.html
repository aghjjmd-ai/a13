The Watch Setting Guide

Setting a watch takes a minute, but doing it in the wrong order can strain the movement. Here is the safe way, step by step.

01 // Know your crown positions

PositionWhat it usually does
0 (pushed in)Hand-winding on most automatics and manuals
1 (first click)Quick-set date, or second time zone on some GMTs
2 (second click)Set the time; seconds hand often stops (“hacks”)

Screw-down crowns must be unscrewed anticlockwise before they can be pulled out.

02 // Setting the date safely

  1. Pull to the time position and move the hands to about 6:00.
  2. Push to the date position and set the date to the day before.
  3. Pull back out and advance the hands past midnight; the date flips to today.
  4. Continue to the correct time, morning or afternoon.

Unsure whether it is safe right now? Use the Safe Date Setter.

03 // Setting the time to the second

If your watch hacks, pull the crown when the seconds hand reaches 12, set the minute hand slightly past the correct minute and then back onto it, which takes up slack in the gears, and push the crown in on the exact minute of a reliable time source.

04 // Using a second time zone

On a GMT watch, the extra hand rotates once every 24 hours. Set it to home time on the 24-hour scale. To read a third zone, rotate the bezel by the time difference: one click per hour on a 24-hour bezel, forwards for zones ahead, backwards for zones behind.

05 // Day-date watches

Set the day first, then the date, using the same rule: keep away from the danger zone and advance past midnight to finish. Some day wheels have two languages; skip past the one you do not use.

06 // After setting

Push the crown fully in, or screw it down until it is snug but not forced. A crown left out is the most common reason a water-resistant watch lets in moisture.
